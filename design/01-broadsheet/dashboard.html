Swiift Crypto Companion

Swiift Crypto Companion

Lead Mobile Developer · Jun 2025 to Jan 2026 · live on Play Store and App Store · NDA on source

An everyday crypto wallet for buying, spending, saving and sending, with instant virtual debit card issuance and bill payments. The brief was to hide the blockchain entirely.

The constraint that shaped the architecture was network quality. Requests hang, then succeed. Every write is idempotent and the wallet reconciles on resume, so a dropped connection never produces a double spend or a phantom balance.

React Native · Expo · Redux Toolkit · TypeScript · crypto rails · Stripe · REST
