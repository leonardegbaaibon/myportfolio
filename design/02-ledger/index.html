Kabu, chauffeur and dispatch

react-native · typescript · redux · google maps · sqlite

A premium chauffeur and car rental marketplace for Lagos. I own the rider side: destination entry, vehicle tier selection, driver hire, and the live trip view. Submitted to both stores in September.

The problem worth writing down is battery. Continuous high-accuracy GPS on both the driver and the passenger device is the fastest way to get uninstalled. Adaptive sampling tied to trip state, plus moving the subscription off the render path, brought drain down by roughly two fifths without losing dispatch precision.

how i work. six things i actually do, not a philosophy.

the slow device is the real device

A mid-range Android stays in the loop from day one. Simulators lie about battery, thermal throttling and network. Both the Kabu battery work and the NidiDrive load-time work came out of testing on the cheap phone rather than the fast one.

assume the connection drops mid-transaction

Idempotent writes, a reconciliation pass on resume, and never letting a spinner be the only state a screen can hold. This is not defensive programming, it is the normal case in the markets I build for.

instrument first, then optimise

Sentry, crash grouping and a small set of funnel events go in before any performance work starts. The 68% memory reduction on BlockEx came out of a heap snapshot. Without a measurement, performance work is decoration.

store review is part of the estimate

Provisioning, phased rollout, review rejections, privacy manifests. I have taken six apps through Apple review and eight through Play, and I plan around it rather than discover it in week ten.

design is not a later phase

I work in Figma before the editor and keep tokens in the repo so the two do not drift. Most of the retention I have measured came from reworking a flow, not from adding a screen.

write the handover before you need it

Contracts end. Mine end with an architecture note, a deployment runbook, and the next three things I would do in priority order. Talstrike V2 was scoped that way before I handed it on.
